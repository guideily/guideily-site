FAQ

Is this a retainer?

No. Fixed packs, prepaid, fixed deliverables. When the pack is done, you’re done — unless you buy another.

Do you guarantee meetings show up?

We book confirmed intros. Confirmed no-shows and cancellations are replaced in-window. We don’t guarantee closed revenue.

Can I customize the ICP?

Yes — that’s the intake. Off-ICP contacts don’t count toward the 100 / 5.

What if emails bounce?

Hard bounces in the Lead Pack get replacements within the delivery window.

Who owns the data?

You own the delivered list and meeting notes. We don’t resell your ICP list to competitors in the same niche.

How fast is delivery?

Lead Pack: about 5 business days after ICP intake. Meeting Pack: scoped at kickoff based on your calendar and ICP difficulty.
