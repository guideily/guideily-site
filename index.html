FAQ

Straight answers.

What’s in the Lead Pack CSV?

Up to 100 contacts matched to your ICP: name, title, company, work email, and LinkedIn URL when available. We verify before delivery — this is not a raw scrape.

What counts as a “booked intro” in the Meeting Pack?

A confirmed meeting on the calendar with a prospect who matches your ICP. No-shows get a replacement attempt within the pack window.

Is this a monthly retainer?

No. Each pack is a one-time prepaid purchase with a defined deliverable. Buy another when you need more pipeline.
