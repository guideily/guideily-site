FAQ

Straight answers

Why start with a $149 sample?

So you can judge the quality before spending more. You get 20 real contacts that match your ICP. If you move to a Lead Pack or Outbound Pack, the $149 is taken off the price.

What’s in the CSV?

Name, title, company, company website, location, company size, work email, email check result, LinkedIn URL when available, and why each contact fits your ICP. Lead Packs also include a personalized first line per contact.

How are the leads found and checked?

We build each list from B2B contact databases and public sources such as company websites and LinkedIn, then check each person’s current title and company and run every email through an email verification service. We use software to speed up research, and we check each row against your ICP before it ships.

What does the Outbound Pack include?

One campaign: a verified lead list, a 3–4 step email sequence written for your offer, and us running the sends and follow-ups. Interested replies go straight to you. We don’t promise a set number of meetings, because that depends on your offer and market. You get a clear report of what was sent and what came back.

What if the list quality is off?

If fewer than 80% of your sample matches the ICP we agreed on, you get a full refund. On any pack, emails that bounce within 14 days of delivery are replaced free. Details are in our refund policy, sent with every order.

Is this a monthly retainer?

No. Each pack is a one-time purchase with a defined deliverable. Order another when you need more.

How do I pay?

Online checkout is coming soon. Send a request using the form below and we’ll reply with a secure payment link after confirming your ICP.

Do you sell to students or edtech?

No. Guideily packs are for US B2B agencies, boutique SaaS, and recruiters. Our previous edtech product is archived; see the footer.
